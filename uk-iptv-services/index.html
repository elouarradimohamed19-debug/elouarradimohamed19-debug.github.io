UK IPTV Services Compared: Three Options for British Households

Updated 8 November 2026

British viewers usually weigh internet TV against a Sky or Virgin bill and free Freeview. These three services are aimed at UK homes; here is what each publishes about plans, trials and refunds.

UK services

IPTV United Kingdom

IPTV United Kingdom — Best value in the UK. A single month costs £10, and the longest plan brings it down to about £4.08/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: Ask for multi-screen prices. Trial: Free 24-hour trial on WhatsApp. Refunds: 7 days if it does not work on your device and cannot be fixed, or if your login is not sent within 24 hours.

  • Lowest UK prices here: £10 a month, £49 a year
  • Free 24-hour trial
  • No auto-renewal
  • Keep in mind: Refund only when the service does not work and cannot be fixed

British IPTV

British IPTV — UK plans for up to 4 devices. A single month costs £15, and the longest plan brings it down to about £4.58/month on the 24 months plan. Screens: 1 to 4 devices. Trial: Free 3-hour trial, no card. Refunds: 48 hours if the service does not work as described.

  • Plans for 1 to 4 devices
  • Free trial without a card
  • Keep in mind: Short 3-hour trial
  • Keep in mind: 48-hour refund window

BenTV Pro UK

BenTV Pro UK — Best UK refund terms. A single month costs £15, and the longest plan brings it down to about £4.58/month on the 24 months plan. Screens: 1 to 3 screens (3 screens: £100 a year). Trial: Free trial; length not stated. Refunds: Full refund before your login is used; 7 days after activation if it will not work on your device.

  • 7-day refund after activation if it will not work
  • Orders checked by a person, not a bot
  • 3 screens for £100 a year
  • Keep in mind: Trial length is not given
